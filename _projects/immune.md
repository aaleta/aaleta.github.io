I collaborate with the IMMUNE project — my main role is to provide local
support for the Spanish experiments and the surrounding data analysis.

IMMUNE delivers an interdisciplinary modelling framework that explores how
human behaviour shapes disease spread, through the lens of social sciences:
risk perceptions around diseases and vaccines, (mis)information campaigns,
social-peer influence, and digitalisation.
