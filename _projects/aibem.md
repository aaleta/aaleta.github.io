I am a researcher in AIBEM (AI-Driven Behavioural Epidemic Models for
Intervention Design), led by the University of Zaragoza together with
Universidad Carlos III de Madrid and Universitat Pompeu Fabra, and funded by
the CaixaResearch Health Research call 2026.

The project builds epidemic models driven by generative AI agents, validated
against behavioural experiments, to capture how trust in health authorities,
adherence to measures, misinformation and socioeconomic inequality shape
disease spread — and to design fairer public health interventions.
