I am a fellow of the AccelNet-MultiNet project. In addition to strengthening
my relationships with peers in the US, I take part in the various events
and meetings organised across the network.

AccelNet-MultiNet is an NSF-supported joint initiative led by the Network
Science Institutes of Northeastern University (NetSI) and Indiana University
(IUNI). Its purpose is to expand the international community engaged in
scientific discovery and training related to the exploration of multilayer
network science.
