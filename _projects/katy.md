I was part of the team working on the KATY project at the University of
Zaragoza. We used our expertise in network and systems biology to predict
cellular and network perturbations from transcriptomic data, and to
integrate network methodologies with AI techniques.

KATY was funded by the European Union to build an AI-empowered personalised
medicine system, putting easily-interpretable AI data at clinicians' and
researchers' fingertips. As a stress test, the project initially focused on
patients diagnosed with a rare and complex form of kidney cancer.
