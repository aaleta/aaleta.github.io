I am the co-IP of the HIDDEN-NETS project, funded by MICIU/AEI/10.13039/501100011033.

The project bundles several research lines: the theoretical study of
higher-order interactions in complex systems, the characterisation of
data-driven complex systems, and the study of emergent properties in AI
models. Rather than isolating ourselves within individual fields, we weave
expertise across domains into a cohesive team — the shared aim is a more
comprehensive understanding of complex systems.
