I am a physicist and computer scientist at the [Institute for Biocomputation and Physics of Complex Systems (BIFI)]({{ site.institute_url }}) in Zaragoza. My research is centered on data-rich complex systems. I use a mix of physics, data science, and (like everyone else these days) AI to understand the true dynamics generating that data. When I'm not doing that, I tinker with software projects like my VR space golf game, [Astro Putt](https://github.com/aaleta/astro_putt).
