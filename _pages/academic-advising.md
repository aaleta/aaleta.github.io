## Tools I've built for the role

- **[Reminders Bot](https://github.com/aaleta/bot_tutorial)** — a small
  Telegram bot that fires scheduled reminders. I use it to nudge students
  about upcoming deadlines.

- **[Degree in Physics Dashboard](https://aaleta.github.io/unizar_dashboard/)** —
  a web app that puts the official statistics of the degree in one place:
  grade distributions, pass and no-show rates per course, admission marks,
  timetables and exam dates. The numbers were always public, but scattered
  across spreadsheets and reports; now students can check them before
  enrolling instead of relying on hallway rumours. Built by a student as
  an external internship at BIFI. Source on
  [GitHub](https://github.com/aaleta/unizar_dashboard).
